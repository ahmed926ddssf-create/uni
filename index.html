engineering majors



mechanical engineer is a professional who designs, analyzes, and maintains mechanical systems. These systems can include engines, machines, tools, and even heating and cooling systems. Mechanical engineers use principles of physics, mathematics, and materials science to create efficient and reliable products. They work in many industries such as automotive, aerospace, energy, and manufacturing. Their tasks often involve designing parts using computer software, testing prototypes, and improving existing systems to make them more efficient and safe. Mechanical engineers also play an important role in innovation, helping develop new technologies that improve everyday life, from renewable energy systems to advanced robotics. Overall, they are essential in turning ideas into practical, working solutions


An electrical engineer is a professional who designs, develops, and maintains electrical and electronic systems. These systems include power generation, transmission, and distribution, as well as devices like circuits, motors, and communication equipment. Electrical engineers use principles of electricity, physics, and mathematics to solve problems and create efficient systems. They often work with tools like circuit design software and testing equipment to ensure everything works safely and reliably


civil engineer is a professional who designs, builds, and maintains infrastructure projects that are essential for everyday life. These projects include roads, bridges, buildings, dams, and water supply systems. Civil engineers use principles of physics, mathematics, and engineering to ensure that structures are safe, stable, and durable. They study the strength of materials, environmental conditions, and loads that structures will carry.
